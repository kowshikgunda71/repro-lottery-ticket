# Replication (pre-registered, results pending) — Lottery Ticket Hypothesis on CIFAR-10

Independent replication of **Section 3** of Frankle & Carbin, *The Lottery Ticket
Hypothesis: Finding Sparse, Trainable Neural Networks* (ICLR 2019,
[arXiv:1803.03635](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03635)) — the Conv-2/4/6 results.

**Status: pre-registered, not yet run.** [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md)
and [claims.json](claims.json) were published *before* any training happened.
Results will be added here whatever they show, including the two claims already
predicted to fail.

Companion to the completed MNIST half:
[repro-lottery-ticket-lenet](https://github.com/kowshikgunda71/repro-lottery-ticket-lenet)
(6/7 claims replicated across 3 seeds).

All credit for the original research belongs to the paper's authors. This
repository is an independent replication written from the paper's text; it uses
and redistributes none of their code, data, or models. CIFAR-10 is read from
Krizhevsky (2009) and is not redistributed here.

Built with [paper-repro-gym](https://github.com/kowshikgunda71/paper-repro-gym).
